# Context to give a writer

Copy what the guide needs into its context file.

## The reader

Someone starting or running a group: a club, a team, a community. They
have an account in the Atmosphere (Bluesky's network), a handle such as
`@alice.bsky.social`, and have used apps on it. They do not know what a
PDS, a DID or a space is, and do not need to: a guide names what they
see and what they do. They came to the page to get one thing done, and
read it beside the console, a step at a time.

## A group host, in their words

- A group is an account of its own, with its own handle, that the
  people who run it act for. Nobody signs in as it.
- Members keep their own accounts. Joining a group gives them a place in
  it, and the roles they are given there say what they can do.
- The console is the site where groups are started and run. Apps,
  including AI apps, can be connected to a group, and do only what the
  member who connected them can.

## Words

Use the console's words, as drawn: "Invite someone", "Asking to join",
"Settings". When a guide needs one the reader may not know, explain it
in a clause the first time:

- **handle**: an account's name, as `@alice.bsky.social`.
- **role**: a name given to members, such as `moderator`, and what it
  lets them do.
- **app password**: a password made for one app, which can be revoked
  without changing your own.
