# The guides, and the questions each answers

In the order a reader asks them. A guide is one task, done start to
finish. These lists come before the walk: drop a question the console
has no answer for, and add what the walk raises. A guide with no list
here gets one written the same way: what the reader wants done, then a
question for each step, then what they see once it is done, then what
they might do next.

## starting-a-group.md

1. How do I sign in?
2. Where do I start a group, and what does the form ask?
3. What is the address, and can I change it later?
4. Who can see my group, and who can join it?
5. What does my new group look like, and what is on its page?
6. How do I give it a picture and words?
7. What next: inviting people, writing rules.

## inviting-people.md

1. How do I invite someone, by their handle?
2. What do they see, and how do they join from it?
3. How do I see who is invited, and cancel an invite?
4. How do people ask to join, and how do I approve or decline them?

## rules-and-roles.md

1. How do I add, change and delete the group's rules?
2. What is a role, and what can it let a member do?
3. How do I create a role and give it to someone?

## moderating.md

1. How does a member report something?
2. Where do I see what was reported, and who can?
3. How do I label or take down a post, and resolve a report?
4. How do I remove someone from the group?

## signing-in-as-a-group.md

1. How do I connect an app, or an AI app over MCP, to a group?
2. What can it do, and as whom?
3. How do I make an app password, and revoke one?
4. Where do I see what is connected, and disconnect it?

## recovery-keys.md

1. What is a recovery key, and why add one?
2. How do I add and remove one?

## moving-a-group.md

1. How do I move an account I already have here to be a group?
